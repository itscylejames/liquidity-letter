⚡ Zeus — The Intelligent Workflow

Zeus is the proprietary intelligence layer built into the core of The Liquidity Letter terminal. It is not a chatbot. It is not a generic assistant. Zeus is a purpose-built financial workflow system that operates across three dimensions:

  • Zeus Intelligence — Daily swing trade setups across 31 assets (crypto, stocks, commodities) scored across 5 technical signals on a daily timeframe. Every call includes entry zone, target, stop loss, and directional conviction
  • Zeus Macro Pulse — A structured daily briefing generated each morning from live market data, synthesising price action, macro headlines, and trade signals into one sharp read
  • Zeus Chat — An always-on macro analyst embedded in the newsfeed. Ask anything about markets, central bank policy, economic indicators, or breaking news and get an informed, context-aware response in seconds

Zeus runs continuously in the background — processing data, generating intelligence, and surfacing what matters. Built for the terminal. Built for the subscriber.
